How to export cookies from LinkedIn?
  1. Install the EditThisCookie Chrome extension.
  2. Open Chrome and log in to LinkedIn Sales Navigator.
  3. Click the EditThisCookie icon → click the Export button (down arrow icon).
  4. The cookies are copied to your clipboard as JSON.
  5. Paste them into the text box above, or save them as a .json file and upload.
  6. Cookies expire — if scraping fails with auth errors, re-export and update here.
